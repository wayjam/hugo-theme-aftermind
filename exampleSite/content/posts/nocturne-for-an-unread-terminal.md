At 22:14 the office floor has been silent for four hours, yet the archival daemon still rotates its logs on the exact minute.{{< note >}}No one reads the rotation digest anymore. The alert channel was folded into an automated summary that summarizes itself once a week.{{< /note >}} Outside the glass partition, the skyline blinks in regular diagnostic intervals.

Writing in a second language slows down the habit of polishing sentences for an invisible committee. Without metrics, view counters, or a waiting feed, a paragraph is allowed to pause midway and stay {{< redact "optimized for clarity" "unfinished on purpose" >}}.

## What Remains in the Margin

When automation absorbs every routine deliverable, human prose loses its pretext of utility and recovers an older posture: keeping watch beside an unattended desk.

- No telemetry beacons, no trending sidebar, no engagement ledger.
- Margin notes remain anchored to the paragraph that provoked them.
- Silence between entries is measured in calendar days rather than missed deadlines.

```text
ARCHIVE_STATE : RUNNING
AUDIENCE      : 0
LAST_HANDOFF  : COMPLETE
```

Tomorrow morning the server will verify checksums as usual. Only the coffee cup and the margin ink belong to someone who stayed after the shift ended.
