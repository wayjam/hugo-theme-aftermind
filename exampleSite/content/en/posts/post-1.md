At 18:05 the PA chime at the end of the corridor still rang twice.{{< note >}}I later checked the building schedule: that announcement recording has not been edited since October 2025. The system simply triggers it on every weekday on the calendar.{{< /note >}} The desk next to mine was cleared out last autumn, leaving only an even coat of dust and the paler rectangle where a monitor stand once blocked the afternoon sun.

I ran a small script I wrote today. Nobody needed me to write it—the scheduler generates a shorter, error-free version every fifteen minutes. Still, I like typing a few lines by hand, the way we used to file daily status notes, just to verify I {{< redact "completed a deliverable" "spent a few hours at this desk" >}}.

```sh
# Archive locally only; do not push to any external node
hugo --minify --destination ./public
```

After writing this down, I closed the laptop. Nobody checks the deadline, nobody asks for outcomes, and nobody will click this URL.{{< note >}}At most a crawler will pass by at 03:00, compress these words into vectors, and move on to the next million unattended pages.{{< /note >}}
