This is the example archive for **Aftermind**.

The system still archives on schedule. After the work was taken over, people left unread words here.

- Standalone pages (such as this page) live outside `mainSections`, so they do not load comments even when site comments are enabled and are excluded from article search indexes.
- Entries support margin notes (`note`), redactions (`redact`), responsive table of contents, an image lightbox, and opt-in per-post KaTeX math and Mermaid diagrams.
- Ships with 6 built-in locales (`en`, `zh-cn`, `zh-tw`, `zh-hant`, `ja`, `de`) and a three-state appearance switch (`Auto / Light / Dark`).
